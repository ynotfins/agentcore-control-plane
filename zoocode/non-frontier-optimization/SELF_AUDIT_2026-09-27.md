3. Success criteria unmeasurable without a Phase 0 inference-traffic prerequisite that the plan never stated.
4. Hash-mode cache treated as future work though it is live.
5. Media and Fusion placed in the critical path though both are optional/billable expansions.
6. New model IDs presented as "add to config" though they require explicit contract + allowlist admission with operator approval.
7. No ownership routing (Cursor owns renderer/Bifrost plane; Codex frames inference contract changes).
